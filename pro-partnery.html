What AOP means for cheese

AOP (Appellation d’Origine Protégée) is a protected designation of origin. For cheese it ties milk, making and ageing to a defined area and a traditional method. Raclette du Valais AOP is more than a taste label — it proves origin in Valais, a verified dairy and the original Swiss quality we distribute.

What you receive

Support from every side

When you join the Raclette Point Original family, you get the full setup and the know-how.

Technology and equipment

A purpose-built, certified all-stainless raclette oven for 8 to 16 portions at a time, or an original Swiss TTM grill. Plus serving tools, stainless spatulas, wooden boards, tables, a bell and branding.

Cheeses of the highest quality, straight from the source

Regular deliveries of original Swiss AOP cheeses of the highest quality and certified fondue mixes at wholesale prices, plus the professional accessories. By season that can be Raclette du Valais AOP from Orsières, herb Barry, a cheese with eight kinds of wild pepper from Madagascar, or Vacherin Fribourgeois. All to original Swiss recipes.

Academy in one day

We train the team in a single day and hand over the Raclette Master certification. The manual covers service to original Swiss recipes, the right temperatures, cheese storage and running the show in front of the guest.

Marketing and the map

The Point goes on the official Raclette Point Original map. The website and the app show the location, the raclette menu and information about the place. We supply artwork for the drinks and food menu, print, social graphics, promo videos and table cards.
