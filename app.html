The app is on your home screen. Open it with the Raclette Lovers icon.

The Raclette Lovers shortcut opens the nearest-Point finder. We only ask for location after you tap.

  1. iPhone: open the app in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. The icon is the Raclette Lovers logo.
  2. Android: in Chrome tap Install app, or use the menu and choose Add to Home screen.
